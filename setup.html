🛠️ Set Up Your AI Harness

By the end you'll have Pi — your personal AI coding-agent — running on your laptop, powered by a free Google Gemini key (no credit card).

You need: a laptop (not a phone/tablet), and about 30–40 minutes.

1. Get a free Gemini API key

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key. Copy the key somewhere safe for a minute.
  3. Do NOT add a credit card. The free tier is plenty for this class.
  4. Never share or commit your key. Treat it like a password.

2. Install Pi (your harness)

Open the Terminal app (Cmd+Space, type "Terminal"), then paste: 
curl -fsSL https://pi.dev/install.sh | sh
If it asks you to install developer tools, say yes. Close and reopen Terminal when it finishes.
Windows needs WSL (a small Linux inside Windows). In the Start menu open PowerShell and run: 
wsl --install
Restart your computer when prompted, let Ubuntu finish setting up (pick a username/password), then open the Ubuntu app and run: 
curl -fsSL https://pi.dev/install.sh | sh
If WSL gives you trouble, don't panic — flag me in class or office hours. We'll get you going.

3. Log in with your Gemini key

  1. Start Pi by typing pi and pressing Enter.
  2. Type /login and choose Google Gemini.
  3. Paste your API key from step 1.
  4. Type /model and pick a Gemini model from the list (the Flash models are on the free tier).

4. Say hello 👋

Type a message and hit Enter:

Hi! Introduce yourself and tell me one thing you can help me do.

If it answers — you're done. You have a working AI harness. 🎉

Bonus: use your harness with this site

Once it's working, you can point your harness at the course site. If your harness has web access, try asking it:

Read this course site's llms.txt and tell me the key dates and how I'm graded.

A machine-readable summary of the course lives at llms.txt.
